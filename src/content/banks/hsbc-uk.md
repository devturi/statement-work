## About HSBC UK statements

HSBC UK is the UK retail and commercial bank of the HSBC Group, headquartered in Birmingham. Because HSBC is an international group, HSBC UK statements often include cross-border card spending and international payments alongside everyday activity.

## What the statements typically contain

- Account details (sort code and account number) and the statement period.
- A **balance brought forward** at the top and a balance carried forward at the end of the period or page.
- A transaction table with date, **payment type and details**, paid out, paid in and balance.
- Short **transaction type codes** next to many entries, with a key elsewhere on the statement.

These are general characteristics. Check your own statements, as layouts differ by account and change over time.

## Common conversion problems

- **Type codes in the description.** Codes like DD, SO or CR are useful for categorizing but clutter the description if they're glued to the payee name. statement.work separates the clean description from the category.
- **Brought and carried forward lines.** These repeat on every page of a multi-page statement and are balances, not transactions.
- **Dates printed once per day.** Several rows can share a date that is printed only on the first.
- **Foreign activity.** International payments and card spending can come with separate fee lines, worth reviewing with our [fees guide](/guides/how-to-spot-duplicate-charges-and-hidden-fees/).

## How to convert it

1. Download the PDF from online banking, or use a scan or photo.
2. Upload it to statement.work.
3. Review the transactions; balance lines are excluded and every row has its date.
4. Export to Excel or CSV.
5. Check the closing balance against the statement.

Transaction type codes make a good first pass at [categorization](/guides/how-to-categorize-bank-transactions/): direct debits are usually recurring bills, standing orders are fixed regular payments.
