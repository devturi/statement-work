## About Bank of America statements

Bank of America is one of the largest banks in the US, headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina, with a large base of consumer, small business and credit card customers. Its statements are a staple of US bookkeeping, especially for small businesses that keep business checking alongside personal accounts.

## What the statements typically contain

- An **account summary** with the beginning balance, total deposits and other additions, total withdrawals and other subtractions, and the ending balance.
- Transactions organized by type, such as deposits, withdrawals, checks and service fees, often with a subtotal for each group.
- Dates in month/day form.
- On many statements, a separate list of **checks** with check numbers.

Statement design varies by account type and changes over time, so check against your own documents.

## Common conversion problems

- **Typed sections with subtotals.** Subtotal lines look like transactions when copied, and double-count if they aren't removed.
- **Checks without payees.** A check line usually shows only the number, date and amount. Matching it to a payee needs your own records.
- **Fees in their own section.** Service fees can sit apart from other withdrawals and are easy to overlook. statement.work flags fee spikes. See [spotting hidden fees](/guides/how-to-spot-duplicate-charges-and-hidden-fees/).
- **Merging back into date order.** Reconciliation is easier with one chronological list than with several typed lists.

## How to convert it

1. Download the statement PDF, or use a scan or photo.
2. Upload to statement.work.
3. Review the single, date-ordered table of transactions.
4. Export as [CSV](/convert/bank-statement-pdf-to-csv/) for import, or Excel for review.
5. Check the totals against the account summary.

## Reconciling afterwards

Because the export is one chronological list with a running balance, reconciling against your books is a matter of matching rows, not reassembling sections. The [reconciliation guide](/guides/how-bookkeepers-reconcile-bank-statements-faster/) shows a quick routine. If clients also bank with [Chase](/banks/chase/) or [Wells Fargo](/banks/wells-fargo/), their exports line up column for column.
