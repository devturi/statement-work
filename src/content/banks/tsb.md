## About TSB statements

TSB Bank is a UK high-street bank with personal and business customers. It was part of Lloyds Banking Group until it was separated as a standalone bank in 2013, so long-standing customers' records can span more than one statement design.

## What the statements typically contain

- Account details (sort code and account number) and the statement period.
- An opening or brought-forward balance.
- A transaction table with date, description, money out, money in and balance.
- Day-first dates, with fees and interest shown in the transaction list or summarized.

Formats vary over time and by account; check your own statements.

## Common conversion problems

- **Different designs across years.** A run of statements may not look the same from start to finish. Template-based tools need re-mapping each time; statement.work doesn't.
- **Missing months.** When records are gathered from several sources, gaps are common. The balance chain between statements finds them.
- **Brought-forward balances on each page** are not transactions.
- **Dates printed once per day** need filling down to every row.

## How to convert it

1. Collect all statements for the period, whatever their design.
2. Upload them to statement.work.
3. Review the transactions; every row has its own date and a signed amount.
4. Export to Excel or CSV and sort by date.
5. Check the balance chain across statements.
