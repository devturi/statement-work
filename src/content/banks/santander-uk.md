## About Santander UK statements

Santander UK is the UK banking subsidiary of Spain's Banco Santander and one of the larger UK high-street banks, with personal and business customers. Some of its accounts pay cashback or rewards on certain bills or spending, which show up as small credits on the statement.

## What the statements typically contain

- Account details (sort code and account number) and the statement period.
- Opening and closing balances.
- A transaction list with date, description, money in, money out and balance.
- Fees, interest and any reward or cashback credits.

Statement design varies by account type and over time; check your own documents.

## Common conversion problems

- **Small credits that aren't income.** Cashback, rewards and refunds are credits but not sales. Without a category they inflate income totals.
- **Bill payments and their rewards.** A direct debit and a later cashback credit relate to the same bill but appear separately; categories help connect them.
- **Two amount columns.** Money in and money out become a single signed amount in the export.
- **Interest lines.** Interest paid or charged is a separate category and easy to overlook.

## How to convert it

1. Download the statement PDF, or use a scan or photo.
2. Upload it to statement.work.
3. Review the transactions and categories, especially small credits.
4. Export to Excel or CSV.
5. Check the closing balance against the statement.
