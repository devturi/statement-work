## About Citizens statements

Citizens Bank is the banking business of Citizens Financial Group, headquartered in Providence, Rhode Island, serving consumers and businesses in New England, the Mid-Atlantic and the Midwest. Its business customers include many small retailers and service firms, whose statements are full of processor deposits and card-related fees.

## What the statements typically contain

- Account details and statement period.
- A balance summary for the period.
- Transactions with dates, descriptions, amounts and balances, often grouped by type.
- Fees, interest and service charges.

Exact layouts vary by account and over time.

## Common conversion problems

- **Batched deposits.** A deposit like "card processor settlement" stands for many sales, usually net of processing fees. Reconciling it means matching to the processor's payout report, not to individual invoices.
- **Similar-looking deposits.** Daily settlements of similar amounts look like duplicates at a glance. Check the dates and references before treating any as an error. statement.work flags likely duplicates for review rather than removing anything.
- **Grouped sections.** Deposits and withdrawals listed separately need merging into date order to follow the running balance.
- **Service charges.** Business account fees can be summarized apart from the main transactions.

## How to convert it

1. Download the statement PDF from online banking, or use a scan or photo.
2. Upload it to statement.work.
3. Review the single, date-ordered list of transactions.
4. Export to Excel or CSV.
5. Confirm the ending balance matches the statement.
