## About Nationwide statements

Nationwide Building Society is the UK's largest building society, a mutual owned by its members, headquartered in Swindon. It offers current accounts (such as FlexAccount), savings accounts and mortgages, so Nationwide statements often show a household's everyday spending alongside regular transfers into savings.

## What the statements typically contain

- Account details and the statement period.
- A balance brought forward and a closing balance.
- Transactions with date, description, money out, money in and balance.
- Interest paid on savings accounts, and any fees or charges.

These are general features; your statements are the reference.

## Common conversion problems

- **Transfers to savings.** Regular transfers from a current account to savings look like spending on one statement and income on the other. Categorize both as transfers.
- **Interest lines.** Savings interest is small and easy to miss, but it can matter for tax. A dedicated category makes it easy to total for the year. See the [tax season checklist](/guides/tax-season-bank-statement-checklist/).
- **Date carried down.** Several transactions under one printed date need the date filled in.
- **Split amount columns.** Money in and money out become one signed amount.

## How to convert it
