## About Wells Fargo statements

Wells Fargo is one of the largest US banks, headquartered in San Francisco, with a large branch network and many small business customers. Its statements are common in US bookkeeping for checking and savings accounts, business accounts and credit cards.

## What the statements typically contain

- An **activity summary**: beginning balance, deposits and credits, withdrawals and debits, and ending balance.
- A **transaction history** table, usually with date, description, and separate columns for deposits/credits and withdrawals/debits.
- A balance column. On some statements the balance appears once per day rather than after every transaction.
- Fee and interest summaries, and on business statements, extra sections for account details.

Layouts differ between account types and change over time; your statements are the reference.

## Common conversion problems

- **Two amount columns.** Money in and money out in separate columns means a blank cell on every row. Copying text often shifts the remaining values left, putting a withdrawal under deposits. statement.work produces one signed amount per row.
- **Day-end balances.** If the balance prints only at the end of each day, some rows have no balance at all. A converted running balance on every row makes checking easier, but compare against the statement at day ends.
- **Wrapped descriptions.** Long online transfer and card descriptions often run onto a second line, which becomes a separate row when pasted.
- **Summary lines.** Totals and summary rows mixed in with the transactions inflate totals if they aren't removed.

## How to convert it

1. Download the PDF statement, or use the scan or photo you have.
2. Upload it to statement.work.
3. Review the table: one row per transaction, one amount column, a running balance and a category.
4. Export to [XLSX](/convert/bank-statement-pdf-to-xlsx/), CSV or JSON.
5. Check that the ending balance matches the statement.
